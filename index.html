The Atelier

Three Sisters.
One Atelier.

3SSS is the work of Sumaya, Salam, and Saabiriin — three Somali-Canadian sisters who grew up in households where the morning ritual began with bakhoor and the air carried oud.

What began as gifts traded between three sisters became a house. Every fragrance is hand-selected from the souks of the Emirates. Every cream and balm is blended in our Surrey atelier under Health Canada compliance. Nothing leaves with our name unless it could leave with theirs.
